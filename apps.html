CashForge - Finance & Ledger Manager

CashForge is built for finance companies, collection managers, and field agents. It automates daily, weekly (Monday through Sunday), and monthly installment cycles, categorizes borrower records, logs daily field expenses, and enforces strict multi-role access control.
